Bee Yard Wingman: privacy policy

Where your records are kept, what leaves your phone and when, and how it's protected. Made October 27, 2026.

The short version. Your records are kept on your own phone or computer. Nothing leaves it unless you choose to: sign in, share with your club or researchers, or send a problem report. Your yards' locations are never shared with anyone (unless you send someone directions yourself, or choose to give researchers a yard's exact spot). No account is needed, and there are no ads and no tracking.

1. Where your records are kept

  • The Android app: inside the app's own private storage on your phone, which other apps can't read, with a safety copy in the app's private folder.
  • The website (on a computer, or on an iPhone or iPad until the iPhone app is ready): inside that web browser, on that device. Our server sends the screens; your records stay in the browser. Clearing the browser's history erases them, and Safari can erase a website's records if it isn't opened for about a week. That's why iPhone users are shown how to put it on the Home Screen and to sign in.
  • Backups: a file you make (Settings › 💾 Backup), with everything in it, photos and yard addresses included, readable by anyone who has the file. Keep it somewhere safe.

2. When you sign in

Signing in is optional. It keeps a copy of your records online, so your other devices (a computer, a new phone, the iPhone app when it comes) show the same records.

  • No passwords. You sign in with a 6-number code emailed to you. Codes are limited (a few an hour), so they can't be guessed.
  • What goes online is a stripped-down copy of your records, with no locations in it: your hive records (hives, inspections, treatments, feedings, harvests, reminders and Bloom notes) and your apiaries' names. Not where your apiaries are: no address and no map position can be read by anyone but you (next point). It goes to our server (Fly.io, in the eastern United States) over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • Your yards' locations are locked on your phone before anything is sent. An apiary's address and map position are scrambled (AES-GCM encryption) with a key that's kept only on your own devices. The server, the club and the Bee Yard Wingman team can't read them. A new device of yours gets the key by scanning a code from one that has it (with Bee Yard Wingman's own camera).
  • Photos and voice recordings don't go online (for now): they stay on the phone that took them. Backups carry them.
  • On Android, the sign-in and the location key are kept in the phone's secure storage (Android Keystore).
  • You're in charge: Settings shows your signed-in devices; you can sign out of one or all of them, and Delete my account (with a code emailed to you) erases the online copy. Your phone's records are never touched by signing out or deleting the account.
  • While we're testing: what's online is cleared before the app launches. Hives you add yourself stay on your phone.

3. Your club

  • Sharing with your club is your choice, yard by yard. Nothing is shared until you turn a yard on.
  • The club gets counts from shared yards (hives, inspections, mite levels, treatments, swarms, queenless hives, losses, honey), with your name so leaders know who to help.
  • Never shared: where your yards are, hive names, notes and photos. Not with other members, not with leaders, not with the app's owners. At most, a zip code area.
  • Your club's admin and the leaders they choose can see the club's member list and the numbers that members choose to share with the club, never where anyone's yard is. Leaving the club, or being removed, ends your license at once; a leader removing you also deletes the numbers you shared.
  • Bee Yard Wingman's team has no access to clubs, with one exception: our two founding clubs, Cumberland County and Hoke County Beekeepers Associations, agreed that Allen Elks Jr and Allen Elks III can see and help with their club pages, permanently, to look after and improve the app.
  • Join codes are 6 numbers and can only be tried so many times an hour, so they can't be guessed.

6. The testing log

While Bee Yard Wingman is being tested, it records what you tap (the names of buttons and screens), never what you type and never where you are. It stays on your phone (2 MB at most), and only goes anywhere if you attach it to a problem report you send.

7. How we keep it safe

  • Every connection is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • No passwords to steal; sign-in and join codes are limited so they can't be guessed.
  • Yard locations are encrypted on your own devices before they go anywhere.
  • No advertising and no tracking.
  • Research and the state: anonymous records may be shared with researchers and the state (such as North Carolina's State Apiarist's office) to help understand and protect bees, only from beekeepers who turned research sharing on. Names and personal details are never shared, and neither is a yard's exact location unless you allowed it in the app.
  • The app's code is kept in private projects, and the key that signs the Android app is kept off the internet.

Bee Yard Wingman is made by Allen Elks Jr (The Bee Guy) in North Carolina, United States, with Allen Elks III, who runs its server. Questions or requests about your information: alelks@gmail.com, or in the app: ⚙️ Settings › ❓ Help & questions.

9. Keeping and deleting your information

  • On your phone or computer: kept until you delete it (a hive, an apiary, or Settings › Start fresh), or remove the app.
  • Online (only if you sign in): kept while you have an account. Delete my account (⚙️ Settings › ☁️ My Bee Yard Wingman account) erases your online copy and your account; your phone's own records stay. You can also email us to ask for it to be deleted.
  • Club numbers you shared are deleted when a club officer removes you. Club officers keep their club's member list (names and emails) for running the club.
  • Server copies for safety: the server keeps encrypted nightly copies for 30 days, so an online copy you deleted is fully gone within about 30 days.
  • Problem reports you email us are kept only as long as we need them to fix the problem.

10. Children

Bee Yard Wingman is made for beekeepers and isn't directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with its date, and big changes are mentioned in the app's What's new.
